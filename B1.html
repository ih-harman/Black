LeBron James: King of More Than Just Basketball

A Black History Month Celebration

Early Life & Family

Born 1984 in Akron, Ohio. Raised by single mom, Gloria. Basketball star by high school. Drafted to NBA at 18. Now a husband and dad of three.

Changing the World

Opened I PROMISE School in 2018 for at-risk kids. Free education, meals, scholarships. Speaks up for justice and equality.

Why He Inspires

His hustle motivates hard work. His school shows giving back matters. His voice proves you can lead and care.
